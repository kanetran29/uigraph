\chapter*{Use of AI tools}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Use of AI tools}

Aalto University asks that the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools in a thesis be declared. I used
one AI coding assistant, Claude Code by Anthropic, running Claude Opus and Claude Sonnet models
(Opus~5.5 and Sonnet~5.5 in the final weeks; [CONFIRM the versions used earlier in the work]), between
[CONFIRM start month] and November 2026. I am responsible for the whole of the result. The assistant was
used in the following ways.

\paragraph{Code.} The extractor and the evaluation harness were written with the assistant. Every change
to the extractor's behaviour was made test first, and the corrections are listed in
Table~\ref{tab:repairs}. The result tables and plots of Chapter~\ref{ch:results} are generated from the
harness's result files, except the tables marked as compiled by hand.

\paragraph{Readings of source code.} The assistant read application sources in three roles. As a
reviewer it read the constructs behind an edge and reported disagreements with the extractor. In
red-team panels it attacked the extractor, the harness and this text; several demotion rules of
Section~\ref{sec:res-round2} were written in response. As an annotator it judged, from the source, the
pairs of screens read in the comparisons with Rima et al.\ (Sections~\ref{sec:res-rima}
and~\ref{sec:res-h2h}): one model (Sonnet) read each pair, a stronger model (Opus) re-read a share of
them, and the readers were not told which tool had proposed a pair. I re-read a seeded sample of
\HHAuthorSample{} of the readings behind the head-to-head figures against the source and agreed with
\HHAuthorAgree{}; my reading saw the assistant's verdict, so it was not blind. Every defect the
assistant reported was reproduced against the code before anything was changed. All these readings
come from one model family, so their agreement measures consistency, not independent correctness.

\paragraph{Literature.} The assistant located candidate sources and summarised them. Every bibliography
entry was checked field by field against the publisher page, Crossref, arXiv or DBLP, and a citation
audit compared each citing sentence with the source; for most entries the claim was checked against the
abstract, for the compared works against the full text. [The earlier sentence about a separate block of
unchecked sources was wrong: no such block exists. Two entries are in the bibliography file but not
cited.]

\paragraph{Figures and prose.} The diagrams were drafted as TikZ code with the assistant and revised by
me. The assistant drafted and edited prose, which I revised. The research questions and the
interpretation of the results are my own; the analysis and the evaluation protocol were revised in
response to the reviews and panels described above, as Table~\ref{tab:repairs} records.
