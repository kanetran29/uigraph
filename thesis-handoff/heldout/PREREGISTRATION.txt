Pre-registration: held-out applications (5 Nov 2026)
Written before any held-out repository was cloned or mapped. Extractor frozen at c42f220 (packages/ identical at HEAD);
no repair to the extractor or the harness is made in response to anything measured on these applications.

Why
  uigraph was developed on the six corpus applications, so every corpus figure is in-sample. This check measures the
  frozen extractor on applications it has never been run on.

Selection rule (fixed now)
  Source: `gh search repos --topic angular --language TypeScript --stars ">1000" --archived=false --sort stars
  --order desc --limit 60`, run 5 Nov 2026. Walk the list in that order and take the first two repositories that:
    S1 are a deployable application (not a framework, library, component kit, tool, generator, template, starter,
       sample or course);
    S2 have an Angular client using @angular/router with at least one nested route (children) and one lazily loaded
       route (loadChildren or loadComponent);
    S3 declare at least one route guard (canActivate, canActivateChild, canMatch, canLoad or canDeactivate);
    S4 were pushed in the six months before 5 Nov 2026;
    S5 are none of the ten candidates screened for the corpus (realworld, alfresco, ghostfolio, kubernetes/dashboard,
       bitwarden, openMF/web-app, angular/components, vendure, gravitee, SAP/spartacus).
  Criteria (4) and (5) of the corpus selection (browser-test configuration, test-and-source history) are not applied:
  the held-out check uses no method that needs them. A candidate whose Angular client is not in the repository, or
  that is not Angular, fails S1/S2. Each skipped repository is recorded with the criterion it failed.
  The pin is the default branch HEAD at the time of cloning; the SHA is recorded in heldout/apps.txt.

Measures (the same scripts and versions as the final version; harness 1.9.0)
  H1 the extractor completes on the application (yes/no; a crash or timeout is a result, reported, not fixed).
  H2 node coverage against the independent route parser (index.mjs), and the parser's blind spots.
  H3 must conversion, recovered share and accounted share from loss-breakdown.mjs, with attribution.
  H4 must edges and witness-check failures (witness-check.mjs).
  H5 attribution: the loss-breakdown with and without --attribution (attribution recovery).
  H6 Oshima and Kishi's stated restrictions (earlier-work.mjs).
  Not measured: the runtime route oracle, the rendered-interface check, the end-to-end check, mutation testing
  (each needs a running application or suite).

Predictions (written now)
  P1 The extractor completes on both (H1).
  P2 Node coverage is below the corpus's (every declared screen has a node on all six), because constructs the corpus
     did not contain will appear.
  P3 The must tier is small, as on the corpus.
  P4 Oshima and Kishi's restrictions admit neither application.

What each outcome does to the thesis
  Every outcome is reported in the thesis with generated macros, including a crash. A low figure is not repaired before
  submission; it is reported as the out-of-sample result.
