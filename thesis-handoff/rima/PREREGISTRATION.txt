Pre-registration: same-dataset comparison with Rima et al. (rima2026e2e)
Written 5 Nov 2026, before any of the seven subjects was mapped by this thesis's extractor.
Extractor frozen at packages/ commit 2aac284 (repository HEAD 35a2132). The reference files and
the subjects' sources have been read only to count the reference's entries and pairs.

Why
  The thesis compares its scope attribution with the mechanism of Rima et al. by reimplementing
  that mechanism (the outlet level) on its own corpus. The user asked for a comparison on the same
  dataset. Rima et al. publish seven Angular subjects and, for each, a ground truth of workflows
  (softscanner-sw/softscanner_fa f33003f, docs/analysis/phase-b/gt/<subject>.json). None of the
  seven was used in developing the extractor, so this is the thesis's only out-of-sample check.

What the reference is, stated before scoring
  Each ground-truth entry is one workflow their pipeline (stage A2) enumerated, annotated by hand
  with its start routes and its terminal route. The entries were generated from their tool's own
  output (field a2Source), so their tool holds every entry by construction; their reported figure
  on this set is test-execution success (213/303 in their ROADMAP, Stage 5), not graph recall.
  The comparison therefore asks: of the screen-to-screen transitions their pipeline found and
  their authors annotated, how many does this thesis's graph hold, at each attribution level;
  and what does the graph hold beyond them.

Subjects and inputs
  rima/subjects.txt: each subject's upstream at the last default-branch commit dated on or before
  the subject's ground-truth generatedDate (their registry pins no commit). The subdirectory and
  framework are those of their registry. node_modules are not installed; the extractor reads
  sources only, as for the corpus. Their ground-truth files are not copied into this repository
  (the repository has no licence); the scorer reads them from a checkout at f33003f.

Unit and counting rules (fixed now)
  - Reference pair: (s, t) for every entry whose terminal is an internal route t and whose start
    routes do not contain t, one pair per start route s. Start routes: expectedIntent.
    startRouteFullPaths, or for the petclinic schema the navigate-to-route preconditions. Terminal:
    expectedIntent.terminalRoutePath, or for the petclinic schema expectedPlan.terminalUrl when it
    starts with '/'. Entries with an external terminal, no terminal, or a terminal among their
    start routes are counted per class and excluded from the pairs. Pairs are deduplicated.
  - Matching: route parameters match whatever their names (':id' = ':ownerId'); a concrete
    segment in the reference matches a ':param' segment of a graph screen (the e2e scorer's
    url matcher, most literal segments wins). Leading and trailing '/' are ignored.
  - Graph side: the e2e scorer's graphHits (e2e-nav/score.mjs): scope nodes expanded to their
    member screens, the top scope to every screen; edges into nodes that are not screens never
    count. A pair is held when any edge, must or may, joins the two screens.
  - Levels: no attribution, rooted (Oshima and Kishi's rule), outlet (the enclosing-routes rule of
    Rima et al.'s specification) and full, each mapped from the same sources.

Measures
  M1 pair recall per subject and pooled, per level.
  M2 screen recall: reference routes (starts and terminals) that match a screen node, full level.
  M3 every reference pair missed at the full level, classified from the source into exactly one of:
       (a) attribution too narrow: the site is read, its scope lacks the start screen;
       (b) site not read: no construct for it in the graph;
       (c) route not read: the start or the terminal has no screen node;
       (d) not a router navigation (window.location, href to a full URL, history API);
       (e) reference entry wrong (the source holds no such transition), with the evidence;
       (f) other, with a written reason.
  M4 graph pairs outside the reference, full level: counted per subject, split by whether the
     source of the edge is the top scope. Each is classified by reading the source as
       real: a UI path from s to t exists in the source (cite the site);
       spurious: no such path (state why);
       unclear.
     All extra pairs of subjects with at most 40 are classified; above 40, a sample of 40 drawn
     with seed 20261105 over the sorted pair list, and the sample size is reported.
  Not claimed: that either tool is better overall. The reference is their tool's own output, so
  M1 bounds how much of what their tool finds this extractor also finds, and M4 what it finds
  beyond it; neither is recall against an independent truth.

Validity checks
  V1 Every subject maps without error at all four levels, or the failure is reported and the
     subject excluded from every figure.
  V2 Reference routes are checked against the pinned sources: a reference route that matches no
     route the independent route parser (route-oracle/index.mjs) reads is reported; it signals
     a version mismatch, and such pairs are reported separately, not silently dropped.

Predictions
  P1 Full-level M1 pooled at least 90 %.
  P2 Outlet level within 5 points of full: these subjects are small and navigate mostly from
     templates and component handlers, not from services.
  P3 Most misses, if any, are (c) or (d).
  P4 Most extra pairs come from the top scope (header and side-navigation links).

Repairs
  No change to the extractor before M1-M4 are recorded at 2aac284. A defect found here may be
  repaired afterwards; the frozen figures stay the primary result and the repaired ones are
  reported beside them, marked post hoc, with the repair listed in the corrections table.

Outcomes and what each does to the thesis
  S1 V1-V2 pass: a short subsection in ch5 (RQ3 evidence) with one generated table (per-subject
     pairs, recall at the four levels, extra pairs and their classes); tab:rw and tab:earlier gain
     one line each; ch6 threats states that the reference comes from Rima et al.'s own tool.
  S2 Any miss of class (a) or (b): reported as found; the in-sample claims stay as they are and the
     out-of-sample figure is stated beside them.
  F1 Fewer than 20 reference pairs survive V2: no ratio is reported; the counts are given in words.
  In every outcome numbers go through generated macros (rima/score.mjs -> thesis/generated-rima.tex).
