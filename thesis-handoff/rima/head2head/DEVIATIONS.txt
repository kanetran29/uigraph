Deviations from PREREGISTRATION.txt (head-to-head), recorded 5 Nov 2026 after the results were read

1. Their tool's run (run-theirs.sh) finished before the preregistration was committed (5bd2de2); its
   output on the corpus was not read until after that commit.
2. Routes identified by declaration site. Their tool records a route path declared through a constant as
   the expression's text (all of ghostfolio, 23 of bitwarden's paths), so the preregistered path match
   would have dropped their pairs for their names alone. route-decls.mts maps each of their routes to ours
   by the file and start line of the route object; where that fails (most of k8sdash, whose lines differ
   by the tool's convention) their path text is kept. The strata by path (results/strata-by-path.json) are
   kept. The mapping can only help their tool.
3. One reading supplied by hand (results/corrections.json): a reader returned a mifos pair under a
   mistyped start screen; its evidence concerned the target, which no code navigates to.
4. Group "corpus without mifos" added to the pooled figures after reading, because mifos's pair count
   (161k of 163k for this extractor) decides the pooled precision.
5. The real pairs only their tool finds were not classified by cause.

Second pass, after an Opus red-team of the first (5 Nov 2026, late)
6. The first pass paired every start route of a workflow with its terminal, but their enumerator
   (task-enumerator.ts:362) sets the terminal of a workflow that does not navigate, or whose target is
   unresolved, to its own active start route; those pairs were invented by the conversion. Only workflows
   with a resolved WIDGET_NAVIGATES_ROUTE or COMPONENT_NAVIGATES_ROUTE step and no unresolvedTargets now
   claim a transition. The first-pass figures (results/) are withdrawn; results-v2/ holds the second pass.
7. Declaration mapping widened: a declaration mounted at several paths maps to the mount whose path their
   tool records, else to the mounts ending in it (their tool does not prefix lazily loaded routes), else
   to every mount. This widens their pairs on mifos (17617 instead of 3796).
8. An only-ours stratum over 1000 pairs (ghostfolio, mifos) is read in two parts, pairs only the shell
   reaches and the rest, 12 each. Pairs already read in the first pass keep their readings; 108 new pairs
   were read under the same rules.
9. Instead of per-stratum Wilson intervals alone, the scorer reports bounds on each tool's precision and
   relative recall obtained by setting every stratum's rate to its Wilson ends; these are conservative
   bounds, not a confidence interval.
10. Not done as preregistered: the graph without attribution as a reference point; V2 is checked against
   this extractor's screens, not the independent route parser; V1 is the workflow count, and start and
   terminal routes were compared on six subjects (petclinic's reference is written with concrete URLs).

Third pass (5 Nov 2026, late), after the red-team's re-check
11. Pairing every mount of a start route with every mount of a terminal route invented moves across
    mounts (a lazily loaded module mounted under /clients and under /loans). Both ends mounted in several
    places now pair only mounts sharing their first segment; only mifos changes. No pair was read anew:
    the second pass's mifos samples are restricted to the pairs that remain (results-v3/), the new
    shared and only-theirs strata being subsets of the old, and the shared pairs that lost their twin
    form an only-ours part estimated from the old shared sample's pairs that moved. The samples behind
    the mifos figures are therefore small (7, 4 and 5 pairs for those three strata).
