Pre-registration: head-to-head comparison with Rima et al.'s tool on the same applications
Written 5 Nov 2026, before any output of their tool on the six corpus applications was read. On their
seven subjects their tool's output has been seen indirectly, through their ground truth, which their
pipeline generated; their run here is checked against it (V1). This thesis's extractor is at packages
commit a9d29df, after the repairs of the first comparison (rima/PREREGISTRATION.txt, DEVIATIONS.txt).

Question
  On the same applications at the same commits, which transitions between screens does each tool
  find, how many of them are real, and how many of all real transitions found by either does each
  find? "Before this thesis" is their tool; "with this thesis" is this extractor.

Tools
  Theirs: softscanner-sw/softscanner_fa at the commit in THEIRS_COMMIT, stages A1 (static extraction)
  and A2 (workflow enumeration) only, run by run-theirs.sh with the tsconfig of apps.txt, dependencies
  installed with --ignore-scripts. Their B stages need running backends and are not run.
  Ours: this thesis's extractor, full attribution level, the graphs of the corpus re-measure and of
  rima/graphs-repaired. The graph without attribution is reported beside it as a reference point.

Applications (apps.txt)
  Their seven subjects at the commits of rima/subjects.txt and the six corpus applications at the
  commits of the corpus manifest. A tool that fails or exceeds 30 minutes on an application finds no
  pair there; the failure is reported.

Unit
  A pair (s, t) of two different screens, each a declared route pattern, parameters matched by
  position whatever their names. Their pairs: for every A2 workflow whose terminal node is a route,
  each start route paired with that route, the route identities mapped to full paths through their
  own route map. Ours: every edge, must or may, between screens once scope nodes are expanded
  (e2e-nav/score.mjs graphHits). Pairs of a screen with itself, external targets and wildcard routes
  are left out on both sides.

Reference and reading
  The pool is the union of both tools' pairs, split per application into three strata: found by both,
  only by theirs, only by ours. Each stratum is read in full when it holds at most 12 pairs, otherwise
  a sample of 12 drawn with seed 20261106 over the sorted list. A reader sees the application and the
  pair, never which tool proposed it, and judges it by reading the source:
    real      a user on s can trigger a navigation that lands on t (a link, or a handler that calls
              the router directly, through helpers or through an injected service, possibly after an
              await or behind a guard), the trigger being shown while the user stays on s;
    spurious  no such trigger exists; state why;
    unclear   the source does not decide it; say what is missing.
  A stronger model re-reads every pair not judged real and every sixth real one; a script checks that
  every cited site exists and holds a navigation construct. Corrections made later are recorded with
  their reason, as in the first comparison.

Measures, per application and pooled
  M1 pairs found by each tool, and the stratum sizes.
  M2 precision of each tool: real pairs among those it finds, each stratum weighted by its size.
  M3 relative recall of each tool: the real pairs it finds over the real pairs found by either.
  M4 the same two measures on their seven subjects against their ground truth (secondary; the
     reference is generated from their tool).
  Estimates from samples are reported with the sample sizes and a Wilson 95 % interval for each
  sampled stratum's real rate.

Validity checks
  V1 On their seven subjects their A2 workflow count equals their ground truth's entry count.
  V2 Every route either tool names maps to a route the independent route parser declares, or is
     reported.

Predictions
  P1 On the six corpus applications our relative recall exceeds theirs.
  P2 On their seven subjects their precision is at least ours.
  P3 Their tool yields no pair or fails on at least one corpus application.

No change to either tool before M1-M4 are recorded.
